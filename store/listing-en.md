## Full description
ONE AYAT — One Day. One Ayat.

Every day, one Quran verse to read, hear, and reflect on.
No account, no clutter, works offline.

HIGHLIGHTS
• Daily verse — locked for 24h, one-tap listen, tap-to-open tafsir
• Smart reminder — verse text inside the notification, auto-skips read days
• Mark-as-read from the notification, 1-hour snooze, streak-freeze (2x/month)
• Offline adhan alarms — 18 cities or GPS, per-prayer toggles
• Streak & journey — x/6236 progress, repay missed frozen days
• Optional downloadable per-reciter offline audio
• Bookmark folders, private reflection notes, history, daily widget
• Hafs (default) / Warsh rendering, Indonesian–English translations
• Dark + AMOLED themes, core features 100% offline

PRIVACY
No account. No cloud. Your data stays on your device.
Location permission is optional (GPS prayer fix only).

DATA NOTE
Ships with a Hafs text sample + community sample translations.
Licensed full datasets can be imported — sources always shown under
Settings → Quran Sources & Licenses.
