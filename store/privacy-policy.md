# ONE AYAT — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06.

ONE AYAT ("the app", package `com.oneayat.app`) is built privacy-first.

## Data on your device
Bookmarks, notes, reading history, streaks, settings, downloaded audio, and
the bundled Quran database stay on your device. There is no user account and
no cloud sync. Backup files are created by you, on your device, in portable
JSON that you control.

## Network
Core Quran features (daily verse, reader, search, bookmarks, notes, history,
prayer-time math, offline audio playback) work without internet. Network is
used only for: optional audio downloads from sources you configure, and ads.

## Location
Optional. Used only when you tap "Use my GPS location" to compute prayer
times. Coordinates never leave the device. Manual city selection works without
any location permission.

## Notifications
Local only (daily reminder, adhan alarms, Friday recap). No push server, no
registration tokens sent anywhere.

## Ads
The app integrates Google Mobile Ads (banner on non-reading screens only,
never interrupting verse reading). Ad delivery is handled by Google under
Google's privacy policy. A future Premium option will remove ads.

## Permissions (Android)
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` — reminders/alarms you enable
- `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` — reschedule local alarms after reboot
- `INTERNET` / `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` — ads + optional audio downloads
- `ACCESS_COARSE/FINE_LOCATION` — optional, GPS prayer fix only
